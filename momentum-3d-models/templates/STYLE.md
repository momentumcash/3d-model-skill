## Shape rules
- Silhouette first: it must read in one glance from the normal game camera.
- Roundness: [sharp / slightly rounded / very rounded]. Bevel = [~5%] of a part's smallest side, 2 segments.
- Proportions: [realistic / slightly chunky / very chunky and toy-like].
- Repeated parts (planks, slats, rails) are regular, at most a tiny bit of variation.

## Detail level
- Small props: [3-8] parts. Medium: [8-20]. Big: [20-60].
- Detail goes on the 3 hero features. Everything else stays simple.

## Triangle budget
| Size | Triangles |
|---|---|
| Small (hand-held, under 3 studs) | 300-1,000 |
| Medium (furniture, under 10 studs) | 1,000-4,000 |
| Big (vehicles, buildings) | 4,000-15,000 (never over 20,000 per mesh) |

## Banned
- [e.g. realistic photo textures, tiny details that vanish on a phone, neon on everything, mixed art styles]
